Creative Designer & Microsoft Office Specialist

Sunday Elijah Charles is a multidisciplinary Creative Designer and Microsoft Office Specialist based in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. With 5 years of advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint, and 3 years of hands-on graphic design experience, Elijah brings a rare dual skill set that fuses analytical precision with visual storytelling.

Operating under his brand Infinity Designs, Elijah specializes in brand identity development, social media design, professional document creation, interactive dashboards, and presentation design. His toolset spans Adobe Photoshop, CorelDRAW, Lightroom, and the full Microsoft Office suite — complemented by technical skills in HTML, CSS, data analysis, and project management.

Currently pursuing a degree in Software Engineering at the Federal University of Technology, Ikot Abasi, Elijah is committed to continuous growth at the intersection of design, technology, and business strategy. Every project he delivers reflects his belief that great design isn't just seen — it's felt.
